Child Abuse & Child Labour Reporting


Every child has the right to live safely, study, and grow with dignity. Child abuse and child labour are serious crimes under Indian law. Anyone can report such cases — you do not need to be a victim or a family member.



🛑 What is Child Abuse?

Child abuse means harming a child physically, mentally, emotionally, or sexually. It also includes neglect, harassment, or exploitation of a child.


Types of Child Abuse

  • Physical Abuse: Beating, hitting, burning, or causing injury
  • Sexual Abuse: Any sexual activity involving a child
  • Emotional Abuse: Threats, insults, humiliation, or mental torture
  • Neglect: Not providing food, care, shelter, or education


⛔ What is Child Labour?

Child labour means employing children below the legal age for work. Forcing children to work in factories, hotels, shops, farms, construction sites, or as domestic workers is illegal.


Children below 14 years cannot be employed for any work. Hazardous work is strictly prohibited for all children.



⚖️ Laws Protecting Children in India

  • POCSO Act – Protection of Children from Sexual Offences
  • Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act
  • Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act
  • Right to Education Act


🚨 How to Report Child Abuse or Child Labour


1️⃣ Call Childline – 1098

Childline 1098 is a 24×7 emergency helpline for children in distress. Anyone can call this number to report child abuse, child labour, trafficking, or any danger to a child.

📞 Childline Emergency Number: 1098
📞 Call Childline 1098 Now



2️⃣ Report Online – Childline India

You can also get help or information through the official Childline India website.

🔗 Official Link: https://www.childlineindia.org/



3️⃣ Report Child Labour Online (PENCIL Portal)

The Government of India provides the PENCIL portal to report cases of child labour, track rescue operations, and rehabilitation of children.

🔗 Official Link: https://pencil.gov.in/



4️⃣ Approach Local Authorities

  • Nearest Police Station
  • Child Welfare Committee (CWC)
  • District Child Protection Unit (DCPU)
  • School teachers or Anganwadi workers


🧠 Why Reporting is Important?

  • Rescues children from dangerous situations
  • Ensures access to education and rehabilitation
  • Punishes offenders and prevents future abuse
  • Protects children’s fundamental rights


📌 Remember

  • Reporting is confidential
  • You will not get into trouble for reporting
  • One call can save a child’s life
