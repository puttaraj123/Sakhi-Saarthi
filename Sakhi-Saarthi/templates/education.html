Education Support & Scholarships for Children

Education is a fundamental right of every child in India. The Central and State Governments provide free education, financial assistance, scholarships, hostels, and other support to ensure that no child drops out of school due to poverty, gender, caste, or social background.

CENTRAL GOVERNMENT EDUCATION SCHEMES

1️⃣ Right to Education Act (RTE)

The Right to Education Act ensures free and compulsory education for all children between the ages of 6 and 14 years. No school can deny admission to a child based on economic or social background.

🔗 Official Link: https://www.education.gov.in/rte

2️⃣ Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan

Samagra Shiksha is an integrated school education scheme covering pre-primary to senior secondary education. It focuses on improving school infrastructure, teacher quality, digital education, and inclusion of disadvantaged children.

🔗 Official Link: https://samagra.education.gov.in/

3️⃣ PM POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal Scheme)

Under PM POSHAN, nutritious meals are provided to children studying in government and government-aided schools. This helps improve attendance, retention, and nutritional status of children.

🔗 Official Link: https://pmposhan.education.gov.in/

4️⃣ National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

The National Scholarship Portal is a single platform for students to apply for various Central Government scholarships including Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, Minority, SC/ST/OBC, and Merit-based scholarships.

🔗 Official Link: https://scholarships.gov.in/

5️⃣ Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme

This scholarship provides financial support to students studying from Class 1 to Class 10 belonging to economically weaker sections, minorities, and disadvantaged groups.

🔗 Apply Through: https://scholarships.gov.in/

6️⃣ Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme

Post-Matric Scholarships are provided to students studying after Class 10 to support higher education expenses such as tuition fees, books, and maintenance.

🔗 Apply Through: https://scholarships.gov.in/

7️⃣ National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS)

This scholarship supports meritorious students from economically weaker families to prevent dropouts at secondary education level.

🔗 Official Link: https://www.education.gov.in/nmms

KARNATAKA STATE GOVERNMENT EDUCATION SCHEMES

8️⃣ Karnataka State Scholarships

The Karnataka Government provides multiple scholarships for SC, ST, OBC, Minority, and economically backward students through its official portal.

🔗 Official Link: https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in/

9️⃣ Free Uniforms, Textbooks & Bicycles

The Karnataka Government provides free textbooks, uniforms, and bicycles to government school students to encourage regular attendance and reduce dropout.

🔗 Official Link: https://schooleducation.karnataka.gov.in/

🔟 Vidyasiri Scheme (Hostel & Food Support)

Vidyasiri provides free hostel accommodation and food facilities for students from poor families pursuing higher education.

🔗 Official Link: https://backwardclasses.karnataka.gov.in/

1️⃣1️⃣ Minority Scholarship (Karnataka)

Minority students belonging to Muslim, Christian, Jain, Sikh, Buddhist, and Parsi communities receive scholarships to continue their education.

🔗 Official Link: https://dom.karnataka.gov.in/

🎓 Why Education Support & Scholarships Are Important?

  • Ensures equal education opportunities
  • Reduces school dropout rates
  • Supports children from poor families
  • Encourages higher education
  • Promotes social and economic development
