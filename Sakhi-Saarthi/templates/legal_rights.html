Legal Rights of Women in India


These rights are given by the Indian Constitution and laws to protect women, ensure equality, safety, dignity, and justice.


1️⃣ Right to Equality

Women and men are equal before the law. No one can discriminate against a woman based on gender in education, job, salary, or opportunities.


2️⃣ Right to Equal Pay

If a woman and a man do the same work with the same skills, they must be paid the same salary. Paying women less is illegal.


3️⃣ Right Against Domestic Violence

A woman has the right to live safely in her home. Physical abuse, mental abuse, verbal abuse, or financial abuse by husband or family members is a crime. Women can complain and get legal protection.


4️⃣ Right Against Sexual Harassment at Workplace

Every woman has the right to a safe working environment. Any unwanted touching, comments, messages, or behavior at the workplace is illegal. Companies must have a complaint committee.


5️⃣ Right to Maternity Benefits

Pregnant women employees are entitled to paid maternity leave. They cannot be fired because of pregnancy and have the right to medical care.


6️⃣ Right to Free Legal Aid

Women who cannot afford a lawyer have the right to free legal help provided by the government.


7️⃣ Right to File FIR at Any Police Station

A woman can file a police complaint at any police station, even if the crime did not happen in that area. Police cannot refuse to register the complaint.


8️⃣ Right to Privacy and Dignity

A woman’s personal life, body, and identity must be respected. Her identity cannot be revealed in cases like sexual assault.


9️⃣ Right to Protection from Child Marriage

Marriage below the legal age (18 years for women) is illegal. A woman has the right to stop or cancel a child marriage.


🔟 Right to Property and Inheritance

A woman has equal rights to parental and husband’s property. She cannot be denied property because she is a woman.


1️⃣1️⃣ Right to Education

Every girl child has the right to free and compulsory education. No one can stop a girl from studying.


1️⃣2️⃣ Right to Protection Against Dowry

Demanding or giving dowry is a crime. Women can file a complaint if harassed for dowry.



📞 What to Do If Rights Are Violated?

  • Call Women Helpline: 181
  • Call Police Emergency: 112
  • Approach nearby police station or women cell
  • Use National Portal for Women
