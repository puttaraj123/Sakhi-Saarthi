Child Nutrition & Health Information

Proper nutrition and healthcare are essential for a child’s physical, mental, and emotional development. The Central and State Governments of India run many schemes to ensure children grow healthy, strong, and free from malnutrition.

CENTRAL GOVERNMENT SCHEMES

1️⃣ Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS)

ICDS is one of the most important child welfare schemes in India. It provides supplementary nutrition, regular health check-ups, immunization, referral services, and early childhood education to children below 6 years through Anganwadi centres. Pregnant and lactating mothers are also supported under this scheme.

🔗 Official Link: https://wcd.nic.in/schemes/integrated-child-development-services-icds

2️⃣ Mid-Day Meal Scheme / PM POSHAN

This scheme provides free, nutritious cooked meals to children studying in government and government-aided schools. The objective is to improve nutrition, increase school attendance, reduce dropouts, and enhance learning outcomes.

🔗 Official Link: https://pmposhan.education.gov.in/

3️⃣ Mission Indradhanush

Mission Indradhanush provides free vaccination to children against life-threatening diseases such as polio, measles, diphtheria, tuberculosis, hepatitis, and tetanus. This scheme ensures that no child is left unvaccinated.

🔗 Official Link: https://www.nhp.gov.in/mission-indradhanush_pg

4️⃣ Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK)

RBSK focuses on early identification and free treatment of diseases, deficiencies, and developmental delays in children from birth to 18 years. Health screening is conducted in Anganwadis and schools.

🔗 Official Link: https://nhm.gov.in/.../RBSK

5️⃣ POSHAN Abhiyaan (National Nutrition Mission)

POSHAN Abhiyaan aims to reduce malnutrition, anemia, under-nutrition, and low birth weight among children, adolescent girls, pregnant women, and lactating mothers through nutrition awareness and monitoring.

🔗 Official Link: https://poshanabhiyaan.gov.in/


KARNATAKA STATE GOVERNMENT SCHEMES

6️⃣ Karnataka Anganwadi Nutrition Programme

The Karnataka Government provides nutritious food, eggs, milk, and health services to children below 6 years through Anganwadi centres. Growth monitoring and nutrition education are also provided.

🔗 Official Link: https://dwcd.karnataka.gov.in/

7️⃣ Ksheera Bhagya Scheme

Under the Ksheera Bhagya Scheme, free milk is provided to school children on school days to improve nutrition, strengthen bones, and prevent malnutrition and anemia.

🔗 Official Link: https://schooleducation.karnataka.gov.in/437/ksheera-bhagya-yojana/en

8️⃣ Anna Bhagya Scheme (Food Security Support)

Anna Bhagya provides subsidized or free food grains to low-income families. This ensures that children from poor households do not suffer from hunger and nutrition deficiency.

🔗 Official Link: https://ahara.kar.nic.in/

9️⃣ Free Health Check-up & Vaccination (Karnataka)

The Karnataka Government conducts free health check-ups, vaccination drives, and nutrition monitoring through government hospitals, Primary Health Centres, and health camps.

🔗 Official Link: https://karunadu.karnataka.gov.in/hfw/

🔟 School Health Programme

Doctors and health workers regularly visit schools to check children’s physical health, eyesight, dental health, mental well-being, and overall growth.

🔗 Official Link: https://nhm.gov.in/.../School-Health-Programme


🍎 Why Child Nutrition & Health Is Important?

  • Ensures proper physical and mental growth
  • Builds strong immunity against diseases
  • Improves concentration and academic performance
  • Reduces child mortality and malnutrition
